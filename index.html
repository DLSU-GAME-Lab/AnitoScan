Abstract

3D reconstruction of intricate physical assets typically requires extensive manual adjustments or dense sensor arrangements. We introduce AnitoScan, a video-to-mesh geometric processing pipeline engineered to reconstruct 3D topological configurations directly from lightweight video streams. Our framework decouples raw structural estimation from rendering fragments to assemble an organized library of OBJ assets. Evaluated across our custom catalog of 14 figures archived at the DLSU GAME Lab, the current prototype successfully preserves intricate surface continuity, producing high-fidelity output geometries directly optimized for integration into modern engines.

The Core Benefit: No specialized hardware, depth sensors, or complex studio rigs required—just raw, accessible video frames ready for the reconstruction pipeline.
